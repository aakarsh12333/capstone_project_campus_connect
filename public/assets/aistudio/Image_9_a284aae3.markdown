## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a fusion of modern institutional prestige, state-of-the-art cryptographic security, and dynamic campus vitality. Designed for Tier-1 research universities, faculty syndicates, cross-institutional research labs, and student bodies, the visual tone balances cryptographic rigor with fluid, frictionless collaboration.

The visual style is **Contemporary Cryptographic SaaS**:
- **Atmosphere:** Rigorous, clean, and deeply trustworthy. The interface conveys total data sovereignty (MLS/Signal protocol E2E encryption) while avoiding clinical paranoia through energetic indigo hues, luminous glass trims, and confident academic typography.
- **Personality:** Authoritative, razor-sharp, seamless, and verified.
- **Visual Cues:** Luminous keylines, micro-tactile status chips (cryptographic key fingerprints, verification pill locks), rich slate structures, and ambient indigo glows that denote active secure channels.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional gravitas with active cryptographic feedback:

- **Institutional Indigo (`#4F46E5`):** Primary action color, deep engagement anchor, and authoritative focus tone.
- **Electric Violet (`#6366F1`):** Secondary interactive tone for collaborative presence, active shared workspaces, and focus rings.
- **Emerald Verification (`#10B981`):** Tertiary indicator exclusively reserved for zero-trust cryptographic validation, verified E2E channel status, signed identity certificates, and active MLS epoch refreshes.
- **Academic Gold (`#F59E0B`):** Functional accent reserved for verified academic credentials, honors badges, university syndicate seals, and tier-level privileges.
- **Deep Slate Navy (`#0B0F19` & `#111827`):** Primary neutral base providing deep contrast for sidebars, cryptographic state panels, and high-emphasis labels.
- **Surface Foundations (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`):** Pristine slate surfaces calibrated to maximize typographic contrast and eliminate eye fatigue during intensive research and grading sessions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the contemporary geometric clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural headers with the ultra-legible, neutral precision of **Inter** for collaborative communication and metadata.

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Set with tight tracking to convey modern editorial confidence and academic authority.
- **Body & Controls (Inter):** Highly legible across high-density chat logs, code blocks, and academic document margins.
- **Cryptographic Fingerprints (`code-mono`):** Standardizes 64-bit verification digests, public key chunks, and session tokens into fixed-width alignments to allow swift human verification.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural grid with a **hybrid fluid-fixed structural shell**:
- **Application Shell:** A fixed 72px icon rail paired with an expandable 260px channel/directory panel, flowing into a fluid collaborative canvas with an optional fixed 320px contextual inspector (encryption parameters, participant rosters).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** Tri-pane continuous view (Navigation, Active Stream, Security Inspector). 24px column gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Inspector collapses to a slide-over sheet; navigation contracts to a mini-rail. 16px gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Strict single-column stack with persistent bottom-pinned action bar and high-security modal sheets. Margins compress to 16px (`margin-mobile`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines **crisp micro-borders**, **frosted glass tiers**, and **tinted ambient shadows**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Grounded `#F8FAFC` base tone. No shadow, zero elevation.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Module Shells):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill overlaid with a subtle 1px border (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`). Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.02)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Cryptographic Overlays & Popovers):** Translucent backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` fill and a 1px border of `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`. Shadow: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Security Handshakes):** Deep elevation with an Indigo-tinted ambient halo: `0 20px 35px -10px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.12), 0 12px 16px -8px rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a fluid, approachable curvature balanced with structured containment:
- **Default Elements (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem):** Form inputs, code chips, key fingerprint modules, dropdown items.
- **Cards & Surface Modules (`rounded-xl` / 1rem):** Message groups, research project summaries, shared boards.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`rounded-2xl` / 1.5rem):** Verification handshake dialogs, cryptographic key export modals, thread sheets.
- **Status & Identity Badges (`rounded-full`):** Identity avatars, verified security pills, protocol fingerprint chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Security Action:** Deep Indigo `#4F46E5` background, pure white label, `rounded-md` (0.5rem), 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` inset. Hover activates a luminous violet transition (`#6366F1`) and subtle indigo shadow lift.
- **Cryptographic Secondary:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#0B0F19` text, 1px border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`. Hover triggers background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Verified E2E Action:** Emerald tone (`#10B981`) background with high-contrast white text, applied strictly for key verification affirmations and zero-trust channel confirmations.

### Chips & Security Indicators
- **MLS Protocol Chip:** Monospace fingerprint chunk enclosed in `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)` fill with a 1px border of `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` and an Emerald text lock icon.
- **Academic Trust Badge:** Pure gold `#F59E0B` metallic micro-pill featuring the institution seal icon, verifying academic credentials and departmental affiliation.

### Input Fields
- High-contrast white fields backed by a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. Focus state replaces the border with an authoritative 1.5px `#4F46E5` stroke coupled with a 3px ring of `rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Unbordered list rows separated by 1px subtle divider lines (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.6)`). Hover reveals an instantaneous `#F8FAFC` slate highlight with soft 0.375rem internal corner radiuses.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp 18px boxes with 4px border radiuses. Unchecked: 1.5px border of `#CBD5E1`. Checked: Filled `#4F46E5` with an optic-white crisp checkmark glyph.

### Cards & Workspaces
- Surface-grounded `#FFFFFF` container with a distinct 1px hairline border (`#E2E8F0`) and `rounded-xl` geometry. Headers within cards feature an integrated security header strip denoting encryption epoch and verified member count.
